The machine

Every measurement on this page belongs to a place in the model. Here it is, wired the way it was trained. Drag the console at the foot of the page and the instrument moves with you.

Each of the five routed blocks is a bank of 32 experts. Every token entering a block lights exactly two of them, plus the shared expert that sees everything. Blue means an expert took fewer than its balanced share of 224 tokens, red means more. The number under each bank is that block's routing entropy: 1.000 is a perfectly even split, and a collapse toward a handful of experts drives it to zero.

What it writes

The same four words, decoded at every checkpoint with the same seed. This is the part of a training run that no loss curve conveys.

The three middle blocks hold entropy above 0.99 all run and their records are nearly flat: block 04 never puts fewer than 109 or more than 374 tokens on any expert. Specialisation lives at the two ends of the stack. Blocks 02 and 06 sit at 0.95 and 0.96 and swing between 1 token and 862. No column stays hot down the record, and that is the finding: the loss-free bias balancer keeps rotating which expert is favoured instead of letting one win permanently. Gate K watched a block collapse to 0.32 on a far shorter run. Nothing like it recurred across 64,000 steps.

What the run exposed

Three defects, none of them in the model.

The step counter double-counts on resume

This run resumed at step 12,000, and every checkpoint after that reported a step 12,000 too high: the last one logged 76,000 for a 64,000-step run. v3_cli.py passes start_step + step to the progress callback, but the step it receives is already global. Every step on this page is the corrected one, recovered from tokens_seen / 3584, which was never wrong.

No metrics log is written

Checkpoints keep only the last metrics dict, and the history the trainer returns is discarded when the process exits. Scrub to the first three checkpoints and the instrument goes dark: those are steps 4,000 to 12,000, and nothing survives of them but a perplexity somebody typed into the gate document. A twelve-hour run should not depend on a terminal buffer.

Throughput decayed from 3.19 to 1.33 steps per second

Diagnosed at step 28,000 as page faults: 11.2 GiB committed against a 257 MiB resident working set, with the training tokens held as int64 when a 32,000-word vocabulary fits in uint16. 922 MiB that should be 230 MiB. The GPU sat at 72 to 97 per cent utilisation and 60 to 63 degrees throughout: starved, not saturated.
